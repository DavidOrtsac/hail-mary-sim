Your Mission

The Sun is dying. An alien organism called Astrophage is consuming its energy, carried along the Petrova Line between the Sun and Venus. Only one star in the neighborhood is unaffected: Tau Ceti, parked just beyond the compact outer planets for this test. Your mission: get there, find out why, and bring the solution home.

Objectives

1. Reach Tau Ceti — Navigate from Earth orbit (or arrive at Tau Ceti, depending on profile). Use thrust and heading lock to cross the compressed interstellar gap.

2. Orbit Tau Ceti e (Adrian) — Get into a stable orbit around the habitable-zone planet.

3. Deploy Chain & Collect Taumoeba — Press J to deploy the 10km xenonite chain into Adrian's atmosphere. Collect 100 samples of Taumoeba (the organism eating the Astrophage).

4. Centrifuge Enrichment — Retract the chain (J), then press P to spin up the centrifuge. Breed 82.5 generations of nitrogen-resistant Taumoeba over 7 days.

5. Launch the Beetles — Press K to launch John, Paul, George, and Ringo carrying the cure back to Earth.

Time Warp

Interstellar travel takes years. Use Z/X to change warp speed (up to 1 billion x). Warp is altitude-limited — you must climb higher to unlock faster speeds. At 10,000x+, throttle is locked to prevent accidents at extreme speed. When the xenonite chain is deployed, warp is completely disabled — you must manage collection in real time.

Tip: Press B to emergency brake to zero velocity at any time. ESC pauses — you can save, load, or revert 30 seconds from the pause menu. Press Z/X often — time warp is essential for interstellar travel.
