================= DESCRIPTION =================
EARTHSHINE is a living, real-time simulation of Earth, streaming from orbit
around the clock.

Every sunrise, every drifting cloud, every city flickering on at dusk is
computed live as you watch. No stock footage, no loops, no repeats. A whole
planet, simulated in real time, turning quietly below you.

Daylight sweeps across the oceans and rolls into night as the cities light up
one by one. Dawn returns as a thin blue line along the edge of the atmosphere,
the Moon hangs in the dark, and the Milky Way rises behind the night side
before the sun comes around again.

Calm, slow, and easy to leave on. Good company while you study, work, read, or
sleep, and a quiet thing to look up at whenever you need a minute.

Because EARTHSHINE is simulated in real time, no two orbits ever look the same.
